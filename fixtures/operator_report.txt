The basic restart demo now finishes, but we still do not trust deployments.

One approved campaign was submitted twice after the browser retried and HubSpot
ended up with duplicate drafts. A different campaign came back doubled even
though the two submissions did not share a key. When a deployment looked stuck,
someone hit retry in the admin panel and a second set of drafts appeared under
the same approval.

In another case, an operator cancelled after seeing the first landing-page
draft, but the remaining emails appeared later. We also do not know what should
happen when someone edits the selected assets and retries from the same UI
request.

The receipts are the part that worries me most. They say verified, and I cannot
tell anyone what that word covers. Twice now a receipt has said verified for a
campaign whose drafts did not match what was approved.

Please define what the system can honestly guarantee and show evidence for it.
